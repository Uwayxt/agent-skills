# 🧠 Loss Aversion & Framing Patterns

## Psychological Principle
Prospect Theory (Kahneman & Tversky): Losses loom larger than corresponding gains. Users are **$2\times$ more motivated** to avoid losing an asset, capability, or time than to gain an equivalent benefit.

---

## 1. Feature Framing Transformation Bank

| Generic Feature (Low Conversion) | Loss-Aversion Frame (High Conversion) |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Unlimited Project History" | *"Never lose historical revision logs or overwritten code"* |
| "Priority Support" | *"Eliminate costly production downtime with 15-minute response SLAs"* |
| "Automated Backups" | *"Protect against catastrophic data loss with real-time hourly cloud snapshots"* |
| "Team Collaboration" | *"Stop wasting 6+ hours weekly resolving manual merge conflicts across siloed files"* |
| "Custom Domain SSL" | *"Prevent browser security warnings from scaring away enterprise visitors"* |
| "Dark Mode Theming" | *"Reduce eye strain and battery drain during extended late-night sprint sessions"* |

## 2. Micro-Copy for Upgrade Modals & Triggers

### A. Usage Limit Approaching ($80\%$ Threshold)
> *"You've utilized 84 of your 100 free AI generations. Upgrade to Pro now so your automated workflows don't stall mid-sprint."*

### B. Feature Gate (Locked UI Element)
> *"This enterprise filter is locked on your current plan. Upgrade to unlock cross-team analytics and prevent blind-spot decision making."*

### C. Trial Expiration Warning
> *"Your 14-day Pro trial ends in 48 hours. Keep your team's custom integrations active by confirming your plan."*
