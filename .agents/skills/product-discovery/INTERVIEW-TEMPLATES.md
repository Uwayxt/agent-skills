# Interview Question Templates

Structured scripts for conducting generative, evaluative, and workaround discovery.

---

## 1. Generative Problem Exploration (Uncovering Root Pain)

Goal: Understand the user's natural workflow without biasing them toward a particular solution.

1. "Walk me through the last time you tried to `[perform core workflow]`."
2. "What were you doing right before you started that task?"
3. "Where in that process did you feel the most friction or confusion?"
4. "When you got stuck or frustrated, what workaround did you use to finish the job?"
5. "How much time or money do you estimate that workaround costs your team every week?"
6. "How did you know the task was finished and successful?"

---

## 2. Competitive & Workaround Extraction

Goal: Understand the switching costs and limitations of current solutions.

1. "What tools, spreadsheets, or manual processes do you currently combine to do this?"
2. "What is the single best part about your current tool?"
3. "What is the most annoying or fragile part of that tool?"
4. "If you could wave a magic wand and remove one step from this workflow, which step would it be?"
5. "Who else in your organization needs to approve or view the output of this process?"

---

## 3. Solution Concept Validation (Fake-Door / Concept Testing)

Goal: Gauge genuine commitment vs. polite compliments.

1. "If a tool solved `[Core Frustration]` in 5 minutes, how would that change your daily schedule?"
2. "What would stop you or your team from adopting a new solution today?"
3. "If this was available tomorrow for $X/month, would you be able to authorize payment or who would need to sign off?"
4. "Would you be willing to test an early prototype next Tuesday and give us raw, unvarnished feedback?"
