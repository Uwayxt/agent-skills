# 🧭 Project Roadmap & Vision

Agentway is evolving to become the unified operating system for autonomous product design and front-end engineering.

---

## 📍 Release History & Milestones

### ✅ v1.0.0 — Foundation (2026-08-23)
- Initial release with 24 skills across 5 core categories (Product Thinking, UX, UI, Delivery, Growth).
- Interactive CLI installer (`agentway init`, `agentway list`).

### ✅ v1.1.0 — Responsive & Closed-Loop QA (2026-08-24)
- Added Category F: Responsive & Adaptive Behavior (5 skills).
- Added Category G: Autonomous QA & PRD Traceability Loop (6 skills).
- Expand to 35 skills total.

### ✅ v1.2.0 & v1.2.1 — Visual Intelligence & Modern Platform (2026-08-26)
- Added Domain H: Visual Intelligence Engine (`visual-style-extractor`).
- Added Domain I: Modern Platform Patterns (Dark mode, AI Streaming UX, Resilience, Security, i18n).
- Reached **41 production skills**.
- Upgraded A11y to **WCAG 2.2 AA**.

### ✅ v1.3.0 — Expert Automation Engines & Sibling Contracts (2026-08-26)
- Added 4 built-in CLI automation engines (`tokens:build`, `scaffold:module`, `audit:routes`, `gen:playwright`).
- Injected deep sibling engineering contracts across Dark Mode, Error Resilience, AI UX, Color Harmony, and QA Loop.
